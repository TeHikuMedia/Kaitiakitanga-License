# Piki Kaitiakitanga License

By using Piki, you agree to this Kaitiakitanga License.

## What is the Kaitiakitanga License?

Kaitiaki is a Māori word without specific English translation, but its meaning is similar to the words guardian, protector, and custodian. In this context, we protect the data and tools associated with the Piki App using Māori values and protocols.

While we recognize the importance of publicly accessible tools and data, we're mindful that the majority of tangata whenua and other indigenous peoples may not have access to the resources that enable them to benefit from public goods. As tangata whenua, our ability to grow, develop, and innovate has been stymied through colonization. We must protect our ability to grow as tangata whenua. By simply opening access to our tools, data and knowledge, we further allow ourselves to be colonised digitally in the modern world.

The Kaitiakitanga License is a work in progress. It's a living license. It will evolve as necessary to ensure a positive future for indigenous peoples. We hope to develop a license that is an international example for indigenous people's retention of mana over data and other intellectual property in a Western construct.

## Kaitiakitanga of Piki

The models in Piki are built from Māori and New Zealand communities who've opted in to share their data to Te Reo Irirangi o Te Hiku o Te Ika to help Aotearoa build ethical, trustworthy, sustainable, and sovereign AI technologies for our collective futures. These data are taonga, and this means that the outputs, the text generated from speech in Piki, should also be treated with the same level or respect and kaitiakitanga. Therefore, we do not allow users to take outputs from Piki and give them away for for-profit corporations who profit from the theft of our data. These corporations include Microsoft, OpenAI, Amazon, Google, Meta (Facebook), to name a few.

**By using Piki, you agree that you will not upload, paste, share, or export te reo Māori text or speech created from this app into third party products, apps, services, or platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, Messenger, ChatGPT, or Co-Pilot where you’ve signed over rights for them to use, create derived works, or profit from the data you input, share, or create in these platforms.** If you know that the tools you use are not taking or using your data to improve their models, then it is safe to put outputs from Piki into those tools. If you are unsure, please don't hesitate to reach out to us to help, or just don't give them your data.

## Kaitiakitanga of Data Processed

Piki uses the [Papa Reo API](https://papareo.io), which processes your data to enable features such as speech detection and pronunciation assessment. The Papa Reo API has its own [Kaitiakitanga License](https://github.com/TeHikuMedia/Kaitiakitanga-License/blob/tumu/papareo_api.md) which is upheld by Piki. Piki runs the Papa Reo API on device.

1. All recordings are collected and processed on device. We don’t collect any recordings or information about the recordings unless you explicitly share your recordings with us.
2. If you decide to share your recordings with us, you assign us as the kaitiaki of the data that you share. This includes the original recording as well as the metadata created from the recording when it was processed on your device. This metadata includes anonymous information about your device.

## Kaitiakitanga of Data Shared from Piki

Currently, we do not allow or request that users of Piki share their data with us. In the future, we may allow users to share their recordings to help improve Piki. If a user decides to share their data with us, then they do so under the following terms,

1. Te Reo Irirangi o Te Hiku o Te Ika (Te Hiku Media) are the kaitiaki of all data you share with us from the Piki App.
2. Te Hiku Media may use the data you share with us to improve our services and models including but not limited to our speech recognition models(s).
3. We will only use the data you share for purposes that align with the values and tikanga of the Kaitiakitanga License as well as the revitalisation, preservation, and promotion of te reo Māori.
4. We may use the data you share with us for Māori led research around pronunciation.
5. You may request at any time request to delete your data under our kaitiakitanga, and we will do so within 30 days of notice.
6. Models may have been trained with your data even after you delete your data, but no new models will be trained from your deleted data. Note that remnants of your data, embedded in model weights, may be a part of models that are used to fine-tune future models even after your data is deleted.

Please consider these things before opting in to share your data. Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
